PRIVACY POLICY
================================================================================

PriceRight is developed by TheRightHub. This policy explains what information
we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect
----------------------
When you activate a trial or purchase a licence we collect your email address.
This is used to:
  - Send your trial activation confirmation
  - Deliver your licence key after purchase
  - Contact you about your account

Your business data
------------------
All your business data — materials, products, prices, and costs — is stored
locally on your computer in a SQLite database file. This data never leaves your
computer and is never transmitted to our servers.

Licence verification
--------------------
When PriceRight launches it contacts our licence server to verify your licence
status. This check sends only your machine ID and licence key. No business data
is transmitted.

Data retention
--------------
Your email address and licence information are stored on our secure servers
hosted on Railway. You may request deletion of your account data at any time
by emailing hello@therighthub.com.

Third party services
--------------------
We use the following services:
  - Railway — licence server hosting
  - Resend — email delivery
  - Paystack — payment processing

Each service has its own privacy policy.
